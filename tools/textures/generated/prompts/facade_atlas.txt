A texture atlas for a video game, perfectly flat orthographic frontal elevation, absolutely no perspective, no vanishing points, no shadows cast from the sun, soft even overcast lighting. Square image divided into exactly FOUR horizontal bands of equal height stacked on top of each other, each band is one storey of a 1960s two-storey Californian Spanish-revival stucco house facade and spans the full image width edge to edge. The wall is smooth off-white / very light warm grey hand-troweled stucco plaster (neutral pale colour so it can be tinted later), with subtle plaster texture and slight weathering. Band 1 (top): upper floor with three evenly spaced tall double-hung wooden windows with dark green shutters and a simple plaster sill, a thin cornice line along the very top edge. Band 2: upper floor with two arched windows with wrought-iron juliet balconies and terracotta sill tiles. Band 3: ground floor with a wooden front door with small arched top in the centre flanked by two square windows with dark wooden frames. Band 4 (bottom): ground floor with three wide rectangular multi-pane windows with white frames and a low painted plinth along the bottom edge. Each band is designed to repeat seamlessly horizontally: windows are evenly spaced and the left and right edges contain only plain wall at the same height and texture. Windows show dark glass with faint interior hints and a soft sky reflection. Thin clean straight separation between bands, no gaps, no labels, no text, no people, no plants, no ground, no sky.
