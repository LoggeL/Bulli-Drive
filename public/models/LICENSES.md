# Licences: car models

All car models in this folder are built procedurally by the project's own
Blender scripts in `tools/models` (no third-party meshes). The VW logo is the
trademark of Volkswagen AG and is used in this private fan project on purpose.
The licence plate decal ("BULLI", California 1963-69 style) is AI-generated
(OpenAI image_gen via the Codex CLI) and post-processed by the project.

Full provenance, prompts and pipeline: `docs/assets.md` in the repository.
