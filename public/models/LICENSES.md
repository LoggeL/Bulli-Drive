# Licences: car models

All car models in this folder are built procedurally by the project's own
Blender scripts in `tools/models` (no third-party meshes). The VW logo is the
trademark of Volkswagen AG and is used in this private fan project on purpose.
The Porsche 356 carries a stylised crest painted procedurally into its atlas
(shield, stripes and antlers, no lettering, no horse); the Porsche crest is a
trademark of Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG, and the allusion is used in this
private fan project on purpose.
The five licence plate decals (California 1963-69 style, invented plates
"BULLI", "KAEFER", "PICKUP", "356 B" and "THING") are AI-generated (OpenAI
image_gen via the Codex CLI) and post-processed by the project
(tools/textures/generated/prep/plates.py).

The building kit in `kit/` (buildings, landmarks, pier, rails, rocks, the
quay crane) is built procedurally by the project's own Blender scripts in
`tools/models/buildings`. Its atlas combines CC0 1.0 textures from Poly Haven
(stucco, roof tiles, gravel, bricks, planks, corrugated iron, deck boards,
concrete, shutter, container side), AI-generated sheets (storefronts, windows
and doors, shop signs with invented names; OpenAI image_gen via the Codex
CLI, not CC0) and procedural awnings and colours.

Full provenance, prompts and pipeline: `docs/assets.md` in the repository.
